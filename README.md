## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[Probo](https://github.com/getprobo/probo)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getprobo/probo?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/getprobo/probo/stargazers)  

  **Open-source compliance platform for startups**, MIT licensed. **Designed to achieve SOC 2 compliance in just 20 hours** with **context-aware security controls** and **smart task prioritization based on risk profile** . **Automated risk assessment**, **AI-powered policy generation tailored to your stack**, and **pre-filled vendor risk assessments** . **No vendor lock-in** — own your compliance data, export everything anytime . **Tech Stack**: Go 1.21+, Node.js 22+, Docker, mkcert . **Features**: SOC 2 control framework, policy management, evidence collection, user/organization management, control tracking, task management, and team collaboration . **The most complete open-source trust center and compliance platform**. 🏛️



- **[CISO Assistant](https://github.com/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community/stargazers)  

  **One-stop-shop GRC platform for cybersecurity management**, AGPL-3.0-or-later licensed. **Supports 130+ global frameworks with automatic control mapping**, including ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, CIS, PCI DSS, NIS2, DORA, GDPR, HIPAA, CMMC, and more . **Vitality score: 96%** on the EU OSS Catalogue . **Features**: risk management, compliance management, GDPR management, risk quantification, vulnerabilities, MCP, and API . **Controls are reusable objects** — structure work consistently across frameworks . **Docker-based self-hosting** with role-based access controls. All data remains in your environment . **The most comprehensive open-source GRC platform available** . 🏛️



- **[OpenSCAP](https://github.com/OpenSCAP/openscap)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OpenSCAP/openscap?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/OpenSCAP/openscap/stargazers)  

  **Security compliance and vulnerability scanning**, LGPL-2.1 licensed. **Implements NIST's SCAP standard** — XCCDF profiles, OVAL definitions, and CPE matching . **Audits systems against DISA STIG, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, and CIS benchmarks** . **Oracle Linux integration** with `scap-security-guide` package providing pre-built profiles . **Remediation steps** for non-compliant configurations. **The foundational open-source compliance scanning engine** for operating systems. 🎯



- **[QResponder](https://github.com/scorpionus007/QResponder)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/scorpionus007/QResponder?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/scorpionus007/QResponder/stargazers)  

  **Local-first, self-hostable security questionnaire automation**, open-source . **Grounded, cited answers from your own knowledge base** — bring your own model, **data never leaves the host** . **Tier-1 → hybrid retrieval+rerank → grounded generation → faithfulness → abstain** pipeline . **OAuth connectors** for Notion, Google Drive, and Confluence — **credentials stored server-side, never in browser** . **Zero-egress local answering** with local models . **SSRF-guarded website crawler** with depth/page bounds . **The most privacy-respecting open-source questionnaire automation tool**. 🔒



- **[OpenXPKI](https://github.com/openxpki/openxpki)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openxpki/openxpki?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openxpki/openxpki/stargazers)  

  **Open Source trust center solution for enterprise-scale PKI**, Apache-2.0 licensed . **Support for multiple PKI realms** — different CA instances in a single installation . **Hardware Security Module (HSM) support** including nCipher nShield and SafeNet Luna . **Workflow engine** for customizable PKI processes . **Web, REST, SOAP, and SCEP interfaces** . **The enterprise-grade open-source certificate authority trust center**. 🏛️



- **[gapps](https://github.com/bmarsh9/gapps)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bmarsh9/gapps?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/bmarsh9/gapps/stargazers)  

  **Security compliance platform for SOC 2, CMMC, ASVS, ISO 27001, HIPAA, NIST CSF, NIST 800-53, CIS 18, and PCI DSS**, open-source. **Self-hosted compliance tracking** with framework mappings and control status . **Darkbanner** project for teams wanting a lightweight web-based compliance dashboard . 📊



- **[StrongDM Comply](https://github.com/strongdm/comply)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/strongdm/comply?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/strongdm/comply/stargazers)  

  **Compliance automation framework focused on SOC 2**, MIT licensed. **1,303 stars, 246 forks** . **Go-based** framework for building compliance documentation and evidence collection workflows . **Git-based policy management** — version-control your compliance artifacts . 🛠️



- **[ComplianceOS](https://pypi.org/project/autoai-complianceos/)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/...?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/.../stargazers)  

  **Real compliance automation: scans, remediates, and generates audit-ready evidence**, Apache-2.0 licensed . **Key differentiator: ComplianceOS fixes compliance issues** — Vanta and Drata collect evidence; ComplianceOS auto-fixes . **Frameworks**: SOC 2 Type II (50+ controls, 20+ auto-fixable), ISO 27001 (25+ controls), GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS v4.0 . **Scans**: code (secrets, SQL injection, XSS), infrastructure (public buckets, encryption, IAM), configuration (policies, risk registers), and API (GDPR DSAR endpoints, security headers) . **MCP server included** — works with Claude Code and Cursor without signup or API keys. **No data leaves your machine** . 🚀
